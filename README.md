# Atlas Labs Workforce Analytics Suite ⭐

## Project Overview 📝
🧠 Part of my “Data Analyst in Power BI” learning path (17 courses and capstone) on DataCamp.  
A comprehensive Power BI solution to:
- **Monitor Key HR Metrics**: Total vs. Active vs. Inactive Employees, Dept. Breakdown, Attrition Rate.  
- **Analyze Employee Attrition Drivers**: By Dept/Role, Tenure, Travel Frequency, Overtime.

## Data Preparation & Transformation
- **Power Query**: Cleansing duplicates, standardizing formats, enriching with date tables.

## Project Structure 📊
1. **Overview**: Totals, hiring trends, dept & role treemap.  
2. **Demographics**: Age & gender distributions, marital status, ethnicity vs. Avg. Salary.  
3. **Performance Tracker**: Individual (Estelle Chung), satisfaction & rating trends.  
4. **Attrition**: Attrition rate by Dept/Role, tenure, travel & overtime.

## Insights 🚀
- **1,470+** employees; **16.1%** overall attrition rate.  
- Largest dept: **Technology**; most hires aged **20–29**.  
- Women outnumber men by **2.7%**; Non-binary at **8.5%**.  
- Frequent travelers have highest attrition (19% of hires).  
- **Estelle Chung**: latest rating “Needs Improvement” → **Recommend** improvement plan.
